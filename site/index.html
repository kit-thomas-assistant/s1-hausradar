02 / The method

No invented certainty.

Public listing facts are checked against their original pages. The link takes you to the source; no photos, listing prose, seller details, addresses or contact information are mirrored here.

“To verify” means observed on a source page, not guaranteed available. Automated Ohne-Makler entries are archived only after two explicit absent responses; manually checked Sparkasse entries remain candidates until reviewed again. Every listing still needs a human check of its actual garden, route to the station, costs and availability.
